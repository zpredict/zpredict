zPredict is a shielded prediction market on Zcash, built for ZECATHON 2026 (Private Markets track). Everyone sees the odds. Nobody sees who bet.

- **Orders** are split in your browser into three XOR shares, one per relayer. A single share is uniformly random.
- **Matching** happens inside 3-party MPC ([polytune](https://github.com/sine-fdn/polytune)): the relayers cross YES against NO at the midpoint and check your balance and your account credential in-circuit. Only your fill (to you) and one public clearing price per market come out.
- **Collateral** sits in a FROST 2-of-3 escrow on Zcash's Ironwood shielded pool. Withdrawals are batched into one shielded transaction, and every signer re-derives what it signs.
- **Outcomes** come from Zcash mainnet block data (or, for mirrored markets, from Polymarket), are FROST-signed by the relayers, and are verified in your browser.

Reading orders needs all three relayers to collude; moving funds needs two. The hosted demo runs all three on one VM, so read it as a working protocol, not a working trust split. See [Privacy](/docs/privacy) and [Status](/docs/status).

## Try it

1. Open [app.zpredict.site](https://app.zpredict.site) and press **Get testnet ZEC**. The faucet sends a real 0.05 TAZ shielded deposit to the escrow for your account; the relayers credit it after 10 confirmations (about 12 minutes).
2. Pick a market and place an order. The app pairs it with its opposite from your own account (a labelled self-trade), so it fills in the next batch.
3. Open the **Relayer View** (`/trade/`): three panels of noise, with your own shares lit.
4. Withdraw: a real 2-of-3 payout to your own Ironwood address.
