title: Markets & resolution
description: Chain-data markets, the hourly rolling market, mirrored Polymarket markets and whose word each one trusts.

A market is `{id, question, resolution rule, close height, resolve height}`. Prices are ticks from 1 to 99 cents. A matched YES/NO pair locks exactly 1 unit of collateral and pays 1 unit to the winning side. Each rule is a plugin implementing the `Resolver` trait from [`packages/oracle`](https://github.com/zpredict/zpredict/tree/main/packages/oracle); a new market type is a new plugin.

## Chain-data markets

Resolved from **Zcash mainnet** blocks, read by each relayer from its own lightwalletd connection (`zec.rocks:443` on the demo). Anyone can recompute them.

| Plugin | Question | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| [`nu7-activation`](https://github.com/zpredict/zpredict/blob/main/packages/plugins/nu7-activation/src/lib.rs) | Will NU7 activate at height H? | YES iff block H's coinbase carries the NU7 consensus branch ID |
| [`ironwood-pool`](https://github.com/zpredict/zpredict/blob/main/packages/plugins/ironwood-pool/src/lib.rs) | Will the Ironwood pool hold ≥ N notes at H? | The Ironwood commitment tree size at the end of block H |
| [`shielded-actions`](https://github.com/zpredict/zpredict/blob/main/packages/plugins/shielded-actions/src/lib.rs) | Will block H contain ≥ N shielded actions? | Sapling spends + outputs + Orchard + Ironwood actions in the compact block |

## Hourly rolling market

[`rolling.rs`](https://github.com/zpredict/zpredict/blob/main/apps/relayer/src/markets/rolling.rs) makes a new market every hour of nominal block time: "Will mainnet block H contain ≥ N shielded actions?", with H one hour ahead (48 blocks at 75 s; 144 blocks at 25 s from NU7 at 3,543,405), trading closing 5 minutes before H, and N the median of the 24 blocks before the window, so YES and NO are about even. Ids are `1,000,000 + window`, derived from heights only, so all three relayers make the same market without talking. Resolved rolling markets stay listed for 24 h.

## Mirrored Polymarket markets

[`packages/plugins/polymarket`](https://github.com/zpredict/zpredict/blob/main/packages/plugins/polymarket/src/lib.rs) mirrors a binary Yes/No Polymarket market: the same question, traded on zPredict's own private book. Each relayer reads `gamma-api.polymarket.com/markets/slug/<slug>` itself, checks the conditionId and that the outcomes are exactly `["Yes","No"]`, and resolves once the market is `closed` **and** `umaResolutionStatus` is `"resolved"`: YES iff the Yes outcome price is exactly 1 (a 50-50 settlement is NO). A proposed or disputed UMA answer leaves it unresolved.

<Callout type="warn">
**The oracle for these markets is Polymarket (UMA), not chain data.** The relayers attest what Polymarket published (each fetches it on its own, and the outcome is signed only if all three read the same), but nobody can recompute it from a block. A wrong or disputed Polymarket resolution is zPredict's resolution. Trading stays exactly as private as on any other market.
</Callout>

The relayers also fetch Polymarket's Yes price and a week of hourly history, at most every 5 minutes ([`reference.rs`](https://github.com/zpredict/zpredict/blob/main/apps/relayer/src/reference.rs)), and serve it as `reference` in `/markets`. The browser never contacts Polymarket; the app shows the market page only as a plain outbound link.

Which markets are mirrored comes from Polymarket too: [`scripts/polymarket-catalog.mjs`](https://github.com/zpredict/zpredict/blob/main/scripts/polymarket-catalog.mjs) takes the top open binary markets by volume (mixed categories, ending between 2 days and the end of 2027 out, no death or violence markets), with their question, category, event grouping and rules, and writes them into the relayer config. It also downloads each market's image once, resized to 112 px; the app serves those from its own origin.

## Signed outcomes

Only a resolution all three relayers computed identically is signed, FROST 2-of-3 with the escrow's key shares, as an ordinary RedPallas signature under the group key. The signed bytes are `"zPredict outcome v1\n"` followed by compact JSON:

```json
{"market":1,"outcome":"yes","height":3543405,"blockHash":"…"}
{"market":11,"outcome":"no","source":"polymarket","conditionId":"0x…"}
```

The two forms have disjoint keys, so one can never verify as the other. [`verifyOutcome`](/docs/sdk#verifyoutcome) checks them in the browser; [`outcome-vectors.json`](https://github.com/zpredict/zpredict/blob/main/packages/settle/testdata/outcome-vectors.json) pins the format for Rust and TypeScript. Without a sealed key share a relayer still resolves, and the app shows "Resolved (unsigned)"; nothing is ever signed with a stand-in key.

## Capacity

One clearing run has `DEFAULT_MARKETS = 24` market slots. The demo uses all of them: NU7 activation, Ironwood ≥ 900,000 notes, 21 mirrored Polymarket markets, and the hourly rolling market. The rolling generator never opens a market past the cap; a 25th open market would need a second MPC run per window. The live list is in [`deploy/production/relayer.toml`](https://github.com/zpredict/zpredict/blob/main/deploy/production/relayer.toml) and at `GET /markets`.
