## Is it live? Is it real money?

It runs on Zcash **testnet**, with markets resolved from mainnet data. There is no mock credit: every balance is a real testnet deposit, and the faucet sends one in a click. See [Status](/docs/status).

## Who holds my money?

A FROST 2-of-3 escrow on the Ironwood pool. Any two relayers can move it together; one alone cannot. On the demo all three run on one VM, so that split is not real there. See [Your money](/docs/your-money).

## What can a relayer see?

Random-looking shares, the amounts and times of deposits and withdrawals, and the public price. Not your orders, side, size, balance, or which withdrawal is yours. See [Privacy](/docs/privacy).

## Why is the price public?

A market needs a price. Each crossing batch reveals one clearing price per market: that a cross happened and at what price, never who, how many or what size.

## Why didn't my order fill?

Orders only cross within the batch they land in and don't rest between batches. On the demo, a judge account adds that counter-order from itself automatically, a labelled self-trade.

## Why fixed deposit sizes?

0.01, 0.05 and 0.25 ZEC. An odd amount would be a fingerprint on chain; other amounts are never credited.

## Can a relayer steal from me or trade as me?

Not alone. Your account's tag never leaves your browser whole, and the circuit only lets a request use your row if its shares rebuild your key. Two colluding relayers could move escrow funds (FROST 2-of-3). See [Threat model](/docs/threat-model).

## Who decides the Polymarket markets?

Polymarket (UMA). The relayers each read Polymarket's public record and sign only an outcome all three read the same. Nobody can recompute it from a block. See [Markets & resolution](/docs/markets#mirrored-polymarket-markets).

## How do I check an outcome?

Resolved markets carry the block hash and height and a FROST signature. `verifyOutcome` in the SDK checks it against the group key all three relayers serve; the app runs it in your browser.

## Do these docs track me?

No. No analytics, no cookies, no third-party requests: fonts, images and search are served from docs.zpredict.site.

## Is this for agents too?
