zPredict is a ZECATHON 2026 testnet demo. Collateral and settlement are on **Zcash testnet**; markets resolve from **mainnet** data. Nothing here is audited by a third party; the privacy audit in [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/zpredict/zpredict/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) is the team's own adversarial review.

## Real

| Piece | Where it runs |
|---|---|
| Browser XOR sharing, one share per relayer | [`@zpredict/sdk`](/docs/sdk), the hosted app |
| 3-party MPC matching (polytune), balances and 128-bit account credentials checked in-circuit | The hosted relayers; [`packages/circuit`](/docs/circuits) |
| Resolution payouts in MPC | Hosted relayers; regtest e2e |
| Chain-data resolution from mainnet blocks, FROST-signed outcomes | Hosted relayers (`[settlement]` on, `GET /group-key`) |
| FROST 2-of-3 DKG, sealed key shares | One ceremony on 2026-10-23 for the testnet escrow |
| Fixed-denomination deposits with encrypted memos, scanned by all three | Testnet escrow; `scan-e2e.sh` on regtest |
| Batched, verify-then-sign Ironwood payouts; forged withdrawals refused | `relayer-e2e.sh` and `settle-e2e.sh` on regtest |
| Hourly rolling markets | Hosted relayers |
| Mirrored Polymarket markets (US midterms), resolved per Polymarket | Hosted relayers; oracle trust in [Markets](/docs/markets#mirrored-polymarket-markets) |

## Mock, and labelled as such

| Piece | Why |
|---|---|
| **Judge mode** credit | 0.25 ZEC of mock testnet collateral without a deposit, so judges can trade without a faucet. Flagged by every relayer and never withdrawable ([Your money](/docs/your-money#judge-credits-are-not-money)). Testnet only |
| **Demo counterparty** (judge accounts, automatic) | A lone judge has nobody to cross with; the app adds the exact opposite order from the same account, a self-trade that nets out. No bot, resting book or relayer-side liquidity exists |
| **Seeded activity** ([`scripts/seed-activity.mjs`](https://github.com/zpredict/zpredict/blob/main/scripts/seed-activity.mjs)) | The operator's judge accounts (mock credit) trade each market against each other near its Polymarket price through the real MPC, so markets show a price and history. They are ordinary orders; nothing is written to the market data directly |
| `pnpm --filter @zpredict/web dev:mock` | Local mock relayers that combine shares in the clear, for UI work only |

## Demo limits

- **One VM.** All three relayers and the DKG run on one machine, whose operator can see everything. Cloudflare only serves DNS ([#88](https://github.com/zpredict/zpredict/issues/88), fixed 2026-10-26). The model needs independent operators.
- **48 accounts** in the balance table; rows are not reclaimed yet ([#64](https://github.com/zpredict/zpredict/issues/64)).
- **24 open markets** per MPC run.
- A fill shows about 25 s after the order on the hosted VPS.
